Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Gundem Cebinde ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the data you share when connecting your TikTok account to our publishing tool.

1. Information We Access

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Used strictly to display your connected TikTok account identity within your publishing session.
  • Content Publishing Permissions (video.upload, video.publish): Used solely to transmit video files and metadata (titles, privacy levels) that you explicitly authorize for upload.

2. Data Storage & Security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored securely in encrypted local/server environment configurations solely to execute scheduled publishing tasks on your behalf. We never sell, rent, or share your TikTok data or tokens with third parties.

3. Revoking Access

You may revoke Gundem Cebinde's access at any time directly from your TikTok mobile app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
